## The rule (NY LLC Law § 206, confirmed against dos.ny.gov)
Within **120 days** of the articles taking effect, a domestic LLC must:
1. Publish a copy of the Articles of Organization, or a notice of formation,
   **once a week for six consecutive weeks**,
2. in **TWO newspapers** — one printed **daily**, one printed **weekly** —
   **designated by the county clerk** of the county where the LLC's office is,
3. then file a **Certificate of Publication** with the affidavits of publication
   from both papers, plus a **$50 fee**, with the Department of State.

⚠️ The published name must match the DOS record **exactly**: `SNOWSERV LLC`.
⚠️ **Failure suspends the LLC's authority to carry on, conduct or transact
business in New York.** It does NOT void contracts or liability protection, and
filing the certificate later **annuls the suspension** — late is fixable.
