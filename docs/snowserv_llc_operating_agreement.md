## ARTICLE 1 — FORMATION

**1.1 Formation.** The Company was formed as a limited liability company under
the New York Limited Liability Company Law (the "**Act**") by the filing of
Articles of Organization with the New York Department of State on
**[DATE ARTICLES FILED]**. This Agreement is adopted by the Member pursuant to
§417 of the Act.

**1.2 Name.** The name of the Company is **SnowServ LLC**. The Company may also
do business under any assumed/"doing business as" name properly registered under
applicable law (including, without limitation, "**SnowServ**").

**1.3 Principal Office.** The principal office of the Company is
**[PRINCIPAL OFFICE ADDRESS]**, or such other place as the Member may designate.

**1.4 Registered Agent for Service of Process.** The Secretary of State of New
York is designated as the Company's agent for service of process. The address to
which the Secretary of State shall forward process is **[ADDRESS FOR PROCESS]**.

**1.5 Term.** The Company began on the date its Articles of Organization were
filed and shall continue perpetually unless dissolved under Article 8.

**1.6 Purpose.** The Company is formed to engage in the business of providing an
on-demand snow-removal marketplace and related technology and services, and to
engage in **any and all lawful business** for which a limited liability company
may be organized under the Act.

## ARTICLE 2 — THE MEMBER

**2.1 Sole Member.** The Member is the sole member of the Company and owns a
**100% membership interest**. The Member's name and address are:

- Name: **[MEMBER FULL LEGAL NAME]**
- Address: **[MEMBER ADDRESS]**

**2.2 Limited Liability.** The Member is not personally liable for any debts,
obligations, or liabilities of the Company, whether arising in contract, tort, or
otherwise, solely by reason of being a member, except as expressly required by
the Act. The Company's debts and obligations are solely those of the Company.

## ARTICLE 3 — MANAGEMENT

**3.1 Member-Managed.** The Company is **managed by its Member**. The Member has
full and exclusive authority to manage and control the business and affairs of
the Company and to make all decisions regarding it.

**3.2 Authority.** The Member may, on behalf of the Company: open and operate
bank and merchant accounts (including Stripe); enter into, perform, and terminate
contracts; hire and engage employees and independent contractors; acquire, hold,
and dispose of property; borrow money and grant security; and take any other
action the Member deems necessary or advisable.

**3.3 Officers (Optional).** The Member may appoint officers (e.g., President,
Treasurer) and delegate duties to them, and may remove them at will. Any such
appointment does not diminish the Member's authority.

## ARTICLE 4 — CAPITAL

**4.1 Initial Capital Contribution.** The Member has contributed or shall
contribute to the Company the following as its initial capital contribution:
**[$ AMOUNT / DESCRIPTION OF PROPERTY, e.g., "$[___] in cash"]**.

**4.2 Additional Contributions.** The Member is not obligated to make additional

**4.3 Capital Account; No Interest.** The Company shall maintain a capital
account for the Member. No interest shall accrue on any capital contribution.

## ARTICLE 5 — DISTRIBUTIONS, PROFITS & LOSSES

**5.1 Allocation.** All profits and losses of the Company are allocated entirely
to the Member.

**5.2 Distributions.** Distributions of cash or other assets shall be made to the
Member at such times and in such amounts as the Member determines, subject to the
Act (no distribution may be made if it would render the Company unable to pay its
debts as they come due or make its liabilities exceed its assets).

## ARTICLE 6 — TAX MATTERS

**6.1 Tax Classification.** As a single-member limited liability company, the
Company is by default **disregarded as an entity separate from its Member** for
U.S. federal income tax purposes, and its income and expenses are reported on the
Member's individual return. The Member reserves the right to elect for the
Company to be taxed as a corporation (including an S-corporation) by filing the
appropriate election with the IRS.

**6.2 Fiscal Year.** The Company's fiscal year is the calendar year unless the
Member selects otherwise as permitted by law.

**6.3 EIN.** The Company shall obtain and use a federal Employer Identification
Number (EIN): **[EIN, once obtained]**.

## ARTICLE 7 — BOOKS, RECORDS & BANKING

**7.1 Records.** The Company shall keep complete and accurate books and records
at its principal office, including this Agreement, the Articles of Organization,
and financial records.

**7.2 Separateness.** The Company shall maintain its own bank and financial
accounts, separate from the Member's personal accounts, and the Member shall not
commingle Company funds with personal funds. The Member shall observe LLC
formalities so as to preserve the Company's separate legal existence and the
limited liability afforded by the Act.

## ARTICLE 8 — TRANSFER, DISSOLUTION & WINDING UP

**8.1 Transfer of Interest.** The Member may sell, assign, or transfer all or any
part of the Member's membership interest, including to a holding company or other
entity the Member controls. A transferee of the entire interest is admitted as a
member upon the Member's written consent (which, as sole member, the Member may
give).

**8.2 Dissolution.** The Company shall be dissolved upon: (a) the written election
of the Member; (b) entry of a decree of judicial dissolution under the Act; or
(c) any event that makes it unlawful for the Company's business to be continued.

**8.3 Winding Up.** On dissolution, the Company's affairs shall be wound up, its
assets liquidated, and the proceeds applied first to creditors (including the
Member as a creditor), then to the Member. Any remaining assets are distributed
to the Member.

## ARTICLE 9 — INDEMNIFICATION

To the fullest extent permitted by the Act, the Company shall indemnify and hold
harmless the Member (and any officer, employee, or agent) against any loss,
liability, or expense (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any act
or omission performed in good faith on behalf of the Company, except for acts of
fraud, willful misconduct, or knowing violation of law.

## ARTICLE 10 — MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

**10.2 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument
signed by the Member.

**10.3 Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the
remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.4 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement of the Member
with respect to the Company and supersedes all prior understandings.

**10.5 Binding Effect.** This Agreement binds and benefits the Member and the
Member's heirs, successors, and permitted assigns.
