# Broker inquiry email — copy/paste, send to 3 brokers

Vince wants everything **in writing** (2026-10-16): no phone calls, so the
answers are on record and the quotes actually compare. The email is written to
make a written reply the path of least resistance — brokers default to "let's
hop on a quick call," so that is pre-empted in the first and last lines.

**Subject:** Commercial GL + Tech E&O quote request — snow-removal marketplace (Yonkers, NY)

---

Hello,

I am looking for commercial insurance for a New York LLC and would like to
request a quote. I would prefer to handle this **by email** rather than by
phone, so I have the coverage answers in writing and can compare quotes
accurately. Everything you need should be below.

**The business**

SnowServ LLC is a software platform that sells snow-removal services to
consumers and fulfills them through independent contractors. Customers order
and pay through our app; we set the price, take the payment, and dispatch the
job to an independent contractor who performs the work. We own no equipment and
no vehicles, and we have no employees.

**Coverage I am seeking**

1. Commercial General Liability
2. Technology E&O / Cyber for the platform itself

**Questions I need answered in writing before I can compare quotes**

1. Does the General Liability policy cover bodily injury and property damage
   arising from **work performed by independent contractors on our behalf**? If
   there is an exclusion for independent-contractor operations, please say so
   directly — this is the central question for us, since all work is performed
   by contractors.
2. Does the policy cover **bodily injury and property damage**, or is it errors
   and omissions only?
3. What are the per-occurrence and aggregate **limits**, and what is standard
   for this class?
4. Snow and ice is a high-loss class. Is there a **snow/ice exclusion or
   sublimit**? Slip-and-fall following clearing or salting is the claim we are
   most concerned about.
5. Can contractors be added as **additional insureds** under our policy, or do
   you recommend requiring each contractor to carry their own policy naming
   SnowServ LLC as additional insured?
6. **Workers' compensation:** we have no employees, but New York may treat
   manual-labor contractors as employees in some circumstances. If a contractor
   is injured on a job we dispatched, what is our exposure, and do you
   recommend a workers' compensation policy or a CE-200 exemption filing?
7. What is excluded that a business like ours would reasonably expect to be
   covered?
8. **Timeline — how quickly can coverage be bound?** Our season begins in
   roughly three weeks and I would rather not operate uninsured.

If it is easier, a written summary plus the specimen policy or a coverage
comparison would be very welcome. Happy to provide anything else you need.

Thank you,

Vincent R. Citarella
Sole Member, SnowServ LLC
support@snowserv.app

## Where to send it

Send to **three** so the quotes compare. Prefer **independent brokers who handle
both technology and contractor risk** — a marketplace is not something a direct
online quote form can price correctly. Worth including one direct online carrier
as a price floor, but do not expect it to answer question 1 properly.

⚠️ Do not soften the business description to get a cheaper quote. Describing
SnowServ as a snow contractor buys a policy scoped for equipment operation that
never happens; describing it as a pure software company conceals the fulfillment
exposure. Either one is how a claim gets denied.
