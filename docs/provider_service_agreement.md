# SnowServ Provider Service Agreement

**Version 1.1**

> ⚠️ **DRAFT — not yet legal advice.** This is a founder-drafted starting point.
> Before you rely on it (especially the Non-Circumvention and Independent-Contractor
> sections, which are heavily regulated and vary by state), have a New York–licensed
> attorney review it. Worker-classification and non-solicitation enforceability are
> the two highest-risk areas.

This Provider Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **SnowServ**
(the "Platform," operated by **[Company Legal Name]**) and the individual or entity
registering as a service provider ("Provider," "you"). By signing below, you agree to
the following terms as a condition of accepting jobs through SnowServ.

---

## 1. Independent Contractor Status
You are an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer
of SnowServ. You control the manner and means of performing services, supply your own
equipment, and are responsible for your own taxes, insurance, and business expenses.
Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship. SnowServ operates a
technology platform that connects you with customers; it is not a snow-removal company
and does not perform the services.

## 2. Eligibility & Ongoing Requirements
You represent that you: (a) are at least 18 years old and legally authorized to work;
(b) hold all licenses, permits, and registrations required to perform snow-removal
services in your area; (c) maintain valid liability insurance (and, if you use a
vehicle, valid registration and auto insurance) throughout your time on the Platform;
and (d) will keep the information in your provider profile accurate and current. You
authorize SnowServ to verify your identity, documents, and background.

## 3. Performing Jobs
You agree to: perform accepted jobs promptly, safely, and professionally; complete the
services described in each job; communicate through the Platform; and treat customers
and their property with care. You will not subcontract a job to anyone who has not been
approved by SnowServ. You may decline or cancel jobs, but repeated cancellations or
no-shows may result in removal.

## 4. Non-Circumvention & Non-Solicitation (Anti-Harvesting)
**This is a material term of this Agreement.**

The customers you meet through SnowServ are introduced to you by, and belong to, the
Platform. In exchange for that access, you agree that, while you are a provider and for
**twelve (12) months** after your last job on the Platform, you will **not**, directly
or indirectly:

- **(a)** solicit, encourage, or arrange for any SnowServ customer to obtain snow-removal
  or related services from you (or any business you're affiliated with) **outside** the
  Platform;
- **(b)** accept or perform off-Platform work for any customer who was first introduced
  to you through SnowServ, whether or not you initiated the contact;
- **(c)** provide a customer with your personal contact information, or collect a
  customer's, for the purpose of arranging work outside the Platform; or
- **(d)** divert, or attempt to divert, any customer, lead, or opportunity away from the
  Platform.

A customer contacting you directly does **not** waive this section. If a SnowServ
customer asks you to work off-Platform, you must decline and direct them to book through
the app.

## 5. No Off-Platform Transactions
All jobs sourced through SnowServ must be booked, performed, and paid **through the
Platform**. You will not request, accept, or arrange cash or other direct payment from a
SnowServ customer for any job, tip excluded where the Platform provides a tipping
feature. Accepting off-Platform payment is a material breach.

## 6. Customer Information & Confidentiality
Customer names, addresses, contact details, and job details are confidential and are
provided to you **solely to complete the specific assigned job**. You will not store,
copy, share, sell, or reuse customer information for any other purpose, including
marketing or building your own client list. Your confidentiality obligations survive
termination.

## 7. Fees, Commission & Payouts
SnowServ retains a Platform commission (currently **30%**) and pays you the remainder
(currently **70%**) of the job price. Payouts are made on a rolling batch (currently
every 7 days) to the bank account on file. Commission and payout terms may change with
notice through the app. You are responsible for all taxes on your earnings.

## 8. Payment Timing
The customer's card is authorized when they order and charged when you **start** the
job. If a customer cancels before you start, no charge is made and you earn nothing for
that job. Payment for completed work is included in the next payout batch.

## 9. Term & Termination
This Agreement begins when you sign it and continues until terminated. Either party may
terminate at any time. SnowServ may suspend or remove you immediately for breach, safety
concerns, fraud, or repeated customer complaints. Sections 4, 5, 6, 11, and 13–15
survive termination.

## 10. Consequences of Breach
If you breach Section 4 (Non-Circumvention), Section 5 (Off-Platform Transactions), or
Section 6 (Confidentiality), SnowServ may, in addition to any other remedy: (a) remove
you from the Platform; (b) withhold and forfeit any pending payouts to the extent
permitted by law; and (c) recover damages, including the Platform commission it would
have earned on diverted jobs. You acknowledge that such breaches cause harm that is
difficult to quantify and that SnowServ may seek injunctive relief.

## 11. Insurance & Liability
You are solely responsible for the safe performance of every job and for any injury,
property damage, or loss arising from your work. You will maintain the insurance
required by law and by Section 2. SnowServ is not liable for your acts or omissions.

## 12. Indemnification
You will indemnify and hold harmless SnowServ and its owners, officers, and affiliates
from any claim, loss, or expense (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of
your services, your breach of this Agreement, or your violation of any law.

## 13. Limitation of Liability
To the maximum extent permitted by law, SnowServ's total liability to you under this
Agreement will not exceed the total commissions retained from your jobs in the three (3)
months before the claim. SnowServ is not liable for indirect, incidental, or
consequential damages.

## 14. Governing Law & Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its
conflict-of-laws rules. The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state
and federal courts located in Westchester County, New York. *(If you later add mandatory
arbitration, replace this section — have counsel draft it.)*

## 15. General
This Agreement, together with the SnowServ Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, is the
entire agreement between the parties regarding your work as a provider. SnowServ may
update this Agreement with notice through the app; continued use after an update means
you accept the new version. If any part is held unenforceable, the rest remains in
effect. You may not assign this Agreement; SnowServ may.

## 16. Communications & Notifications
You agree that SnowServ may send you operational communications necessary to provide the
Service — including job offers and dispatch alerts, assignment and cancellation notices,
and payout and account messages — by push notification, email, and in-app message. These
communications are part of the Service and are not marketing. They are often
time-sensitive: if you disable push notifications in your device settings, you may miss
job offers. SnowServ will send promotional or marketing messages only if you separately
opt in, and you may opt out of those at any time.
