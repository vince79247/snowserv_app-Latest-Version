How it works

No phone calls, no waiting on a callback. Order it like a ride.

1

Request service

Enter your address, choose driveway, sidewalk, or both, add deicer if you want it, and see the price up front.

2

We dispatch a pro

Your job goes to the nearest available provider. You’ll see your place in line and get notified as it moves.

3

Pay only when it starts

We place a hold at checkout — your card is charged only when a provider begins the work. Cancel before then and you’re never charged.

Our mission

SnowServ exists to make winter safer and simpler. When the snow falls, no one should be stranded, snowed in, or waiting on a callback that never comes — so we connect people who need their driveways and walkways cleared with trusted local providers: fast, fairly priced, and one tap away. We're building the most reliable way to get winter handled, one storm at a time.
